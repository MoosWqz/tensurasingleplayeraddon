# Moos' Tensura Singleplayer Addon

**Moos' Tensura Singleplayer Addon** is an unofficial companion addon for **Tensura: Reincarnated** that expands singleplayer progression with self-endowment, Sage to Great Sage shrine progression, the Unique Skill Granter, subordinate skill systems, and Ultimate Skill evolution paths.

The addon is designed to make important Tensura-style progression possible in singleplayer while still requiring meaningful growth, preparation, exploration, and mastery.

## Information

* **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
* **Loader:** NeoForge
* **Java:** 21
* **Mod ID:** `moostensuraaddon`
* **Main dependency:** Tensura: Reincarnated

## Main Features

### Singleplayer Naming / Endowment

Progress without needing another player to name you.

The addon adds a survival-friendly self-endowment path that allows singleplayer characters to become named/endowed after meeting progression requirements such as level, EP, magicules, and skill mastery.

### Sage to Great Sage Progression

Sage can evolve into Great Sage through an exploration and ritual system.

Craft a **Soul Resonator**, use it to locate a **Great Crystal Shrine**, meet the altar requirements, and complete a ritual at the **Great Crystal Altar**.

During the ritual, Sage analyzes the altar, deciphers crystallized soul data, petitions for evolution, and begins the Great Sage awakening sequence.

### Great Crystal Shrines

Great Crystal Shrines generate naturally in the Overworld.

Shrine variants include:

* Temperate shrine
* Hot shrine
* Cold shrine
* Cave shrine

Cave shrines can blend into deepslate environments, making underground shrine discovery feel more natural.

### Soul Resonator

The Soul Resonator is a compass-like locator item used to find Great Crystal Shrines.

It requires Sage or Great Sage to use and points toward the last shrine it resonated with.

Recipe:

* Corners: `tensura:medium_quality_magic_crystal`
* Edges: `tensura:magic_stone`
* Center: `minecraft:compass`

### Unique Skill: Granter

Awaken the Unique Skill **Granter** and gain authority over mastered skills.

Granter allows you to:

* Grant mastered skills to subordinates
* Choose which skill to grant through a GUI
* Reclaim granted skills
* Inspect subordinate skills
* Progress toward higher authority through continued growth

### Ultimate Skill Paths

Granter can eventually evolve into one of two Ultimate Skill paths.

**Benevolent Empowerment**
A heroic authority focused on guidance, shared growth, protection, and empowering subordinates.

**Absolute Governance**
A demonic authority focused on domination, command, seizure, and absolute control.

Each path offers its own playstyle and interpretation of authority over subordinates.

## Useful Commands

Player-facing commands:

* `/getnamed`
* `/moostensura guide`
* `/moostensura guide sage`
* `/moostensura help`

Admin/debug commands:

* `/checknamed`
* `/upgradesage`
* `/upgradesage forceupgrade confirm`
* `/upgradesage force confirm`
* `/moostensura resetconfig confirm`
* `/moostensura reset <player> confirm`

## Intended Progression Flow

1. Become named or endowed.
2. Obtain Sage.
3. Craft a Soul Resonator.
4. Locate a Great Crystal Shrine.
5. Complete the Great Crystal Altar ritual.
6. Awaken Great Sage.
7. Master skills and gather subordinates.
8. Awaken Granter.
9. Evolve Granter into an Ultimate Skill path.
